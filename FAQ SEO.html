Drones cannot fly safely in adverse weather:

❌ HARD NO (Cannot Fly):

  • Any Rain: Even light drizzle damages drone electronics
  • Active Precipitation: Drizzle, mist, wet conditions
  • Thunderstorms: Lightning risk is absolute safety no-go

⚠️ QUESTIONABLE (Pilot Makes Final Call On-Site):

  • Wind: Hard to judge from ground; pilot decides on-site if borderline
  • Heavy gusts: Wind over 20 mph becomes unsafe

✓ OK (Can Fly):

  • Sunny or overcast skies
  • Clear visibility
  • Wind under 20 mph
  • Dry conditions
